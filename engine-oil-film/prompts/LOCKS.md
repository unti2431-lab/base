문구를 라운드마다 고쳐 쓰지 않는다. 바꿔야 하면 이 파일을 고치고 리포트에 변경 사실을 남긴다.

## [ENGINE LOCK]

기준 이미지: R06 S2(1.3~3.9초) 엔진 컷어웨이. Flow에서 참조 이미지(Ingredients)로 항상 첨부한다.

```text
A sectioned inline four-cylinder petrol engine shown as a precise engineering cutaway.
Cast-aluminium block and head, cut faces machined bright silver with fine milling marks.
Exactly four forged pistons with three ring grooves each, I-beam connecting rods,
a forged steel crankshaft with paired counterweights, timing chain running along the left side,
deep oil sump with a round mesh pickup strainer resting near the sump floor.
Engine oil is visible only inside the cut oil galleries, as a translucent glowing cyan fluid.
The engine geometry, cylinder count and camera-facing cut plane never change.
```

## [BEARING LOCK]

```text
A crankshaft main journal, a polished steel cylinder, running inside a plain bearing:
a thin two-layer bearing shell (steel backing, dull bronze-gold lining) seated in a
cast-aluminium housing. The assembly is sectioned straight across the shaft axis,
so the cut face shows complete concentric-looking circles: housing, shell, oil gap, journal.
The journal sits visibly off-centre, pushed toward the loaded side, so the oil gap between
journal and shell is a smooth crescent: wide on the unloaded side, narrowing steadily to a
hairline on the loaded side. The clearance is exaggerated for visibility, but the crescent
shape is the point. One oil feed hole passes through the shell on the wide side of the gap.
The journal rotates clockwise as seen by the camera. The journal never touches the shell.
```

## [OIL LOCK]

```text
Oil is a translucent glowing cyan fluid with faint streamlines that move in the direction of
journal rotation. Where pressure builds, the cyan inside the film warms through amber to
orange-red, strongest on the converging side just before the narrowest point of the gap.
The load on the journal is shown as one thick orange arrow pressing on the journal from the
unloaded side toward the loaded side. Rotation is shown as one thin pale blue-white curved
arrow around the journal. No other colours are used for graphics.
```
