스타일 락은 프롬프트 **맨 뒤**에 붙인다(앞에 두면 뒤쪽 구체 묘사에 밀린다).

## 공통

### [STYLE LOCK]
```
one single continuous photograph, no split, no seam, no panel, no border,
vertical 9:16 frame, photorealistic documentary cinematography, natural physical lighting,
real materials and textures, slight motion blur where things move, restrained colour grade,
no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no badges, no watermark, no HUD, no diagram
```

### [INFO STYLE]
INFO 패스 전용. CLEAN을 참조 이미지로 첨부하고 그 위에 그래픽만 얹는다.
```
Keep the attached reference image exactly: same camera, crop, lens, subject, light, grade.
Add graphics built into the real 3D space of the scene, following perspective and occlusion.
Colour code: teal for air flow and stream lines, orange to red for pressure, force and danger,
white and pale blue for ice, water and cooling, gold for the single key number,
muted blue-white for neutral leader lines. 1-3 anchor points, 1-3 leader lines, at most one number.
No Korean, no words. Only digits and units.
```

### [INFO MOTION 4s]
```
0.0-0.4  clean scene only, camera motion begins
0.4-0.9  anchor points light up on the real structure
0.9-1.7  leader lines and arrow shafts draw in
1.7-2.6  the number assembles, arrowheads appear after shafts complete
2.6-3.4  pulses travel along the flow paths
3.4-4.0  converge on the target composition and hold
```

### [ROADSTER LOCK]
실존 차량을 그대로 재현하지 않는다. 배지·로고 없음, 형상은 기하학 묘사로만.

a low two-seat electric roadster with a long smooth nose, a short tail, a glass canopy,
